## 一、方法论全集 (信念层 / 流程层 / 证据层 / 交易层)

### 1. 信念层 — 世界观与第一性假设

**B1. Capex 漏斗 + 上游挤压律 (核心世界观)**
Mag7/hyperscaler 的万亿 capex 是唯一信号源头, 沿供应链逐层向下漏斗; 但"供给挤压发生在上游"。原话: "I am just supply chain investing" (2025-12-30); "The biggest signal of whether the AI trade continues is hyperscaler spending" (2026-02-06); "Nvidia just prefers to invest in downstream players. But the supply crunch happens upstream" (2026-03-31)。历史分层 (他自述): 2022 NVDA/TSM → 2024 CRDO/ALAB 连接层 → 2025 NBIS/CIFR/IREN Neocloud → 2025-12 TPU 链 (AVGO/TSM/SK Hynix/LITE/COHR/VRT) → 2026 光子学上游 (laser → epiwafer → substrate → feedstock)。"There are barely any companies in history where Mag7 are dependent on them" (2025-07-28)。

**B2. 稀缺层判定 = 毛利转化, 不是产能/战略重要性**
原话: "Capacity measures potential, not profitability" / "Gross Margins > GW capacity" (2025-10-28); "Don't conflate bottlenecks... with stock market returns. What matters is how it translates to material operating income" (2026-03-13); "Being important to US technology (eg. Wolfspeed) does not translate to being a good investment" (2026-03-24)。归纳的完整判定标准 (跨 AXTI/SIVE/LPK/SNDK 案例): (1) 供给侧坍缩到 1-2 家 (垄断/双寡头); (2) 原 TAM 极小的廉价商品突然被万亿 buildout 必需; (3) 价格弹性极低 — 占下游 BOM 零头, 买方"不问价格只抢配额" (2026-01-09); (4) 售罄/排队信号 (sold out until 2027/2028); (5) 必须能变现为营业利润 (提价权), 否则只是战略重要不是投资; (6) chokepoint (所有头部玩家绕不开你, SIVE) vs bottleneck (控制产出分配即定价权, SNDK 不产 NAND 但控 Kioxia 产出, 2026-05-24) 两类都可投。警告: "it's not a good idea to just blindly buy a bottleneck" (2026-01-18, 基本面烂照样不买)。

**B3. 论点与价格分离**
原话: "If the only thing that's changed is the stock price and not the thesis, give it time to play out" (2025-12-15); "Short term stock prices does not change a thesis" (2026-03-24); "Time is the most valuable asset" (2026-01-23); "If earnings were slowing and forward revenue was slowing, I would be selling" (2025-11-18, 唯一卖出基准是前瞻基本面)。锚定市值与运营指标而非价格: "Best thing to do is look at market cap and not the % fluctuations" (2026-03-20)。

**B4. Alpha = 信息合成 + 早于机构定价**
原话: "alpha is when you discover things majority of markets miss. You miss out by the time you wait for institutions to tell you to buy" (2026-01-12); "Alpha lives within: the disconnect between stock prices and operational indicators" (2026-02-26); "My strategy is identifying structural bottlenecks in the AI supply chains before the market discovers them" (2026-03-19); "most returns are typically made before, not after official confirmation" (2026-05-26); "The most returns come from frontrunning institutions before they figure out the next $SNDK. Not following them 3 months after they file" (2026-05-17, 对 13F 的最终态度)。自我定位: "my edge is probably information synthesis and mapping" (2026-02-21); 中文原话: "凭主观判断操作…很大程度上取决于能否准确预判下一次的技术架构演进" (2026-05-31)。

**B5. 财报是确认, 不是信息**
原话: "Earnings are just confirmation that your estimates before were right... All the profit to be made is before information is known and priced in" (2026-03-21); "This is the period where I sit back and let markets price in the research I've done" (2026-02-22)。市场前瞻 8-18 个月定价, 关键变量是"市场愿意往前看多远" (2025-11-19 SNAP/NBIS 1 年 vs RKLB 到 2028; 2026-03-13)。

**B6. 股权/融资结构 = 第一道否决项 (任何论点之前)**
归纳: 看任何小票第一步查 ATM/EPFA/可转债/解禁/SBC/债息。原话: "#1 thing is toxic financing structure/float dynamics" (2026-04-02); "ABOVE current price funding is a bullish tell" (2025-09-27); "DILUTION IS DIFFERENT. ATMs are different than convertible notes that are different than loans" (2026-03-13)。良性稀释 (建产能一次说清, AAOI; 高于市价转债, CIFR) vs 掠夺性稀释 (逢反弹卖 ATM, IREN $6B; 市价 95 折无限增发, NUAI EPFA)。"Retail investors should care more about the value of their own stock increasing over the company's value" (2026-03-08); "It's better to let all existing bagholders get wiped out first, then go long after the ATM is finished" (2026-04 IREN)。自己持仓也照杀: AXTI 提案增发 50M 股 "若通过我个人不会再持有" (2026-04-06)。

**B7. 散户恐慌 = 机构吸筹; 负面舆论浪潮 = 机构要流动性**
原话: "Retail is last to sell, last to buy back... missing one or two huge rebound days after a selloff can cost you most of the recovery gains" (2025-11-14)。机制模型 (归纳): 算法误读 → MM 对冲放大 → 散户割肉 → 暗池/大宗接货; 验证靠 13F 持股比例变化 (NBIS 38%→48.8% 同期股价 -35%, 2025-11)。2026 年升级为反向信号: 卖方黑稿/降级密集出现 = 吸筹 (Citi 黑 SOI 后 MS 买 6.5% 流通盘; BofA 称 EWY 泡沫后内存新高; SIVE 瑞典媒体黑稿 + bot farm, 2026-04~06)。"Institutions are not your friends" (2026-05-02, Bernstein INTC $35 PT 案)。

**B8. 反 TA, 数字优先于叙事, 但承认"光学"驱动算法**
原话: "Float & fundamentals > lines on a chart" (2025-09-16); "TA and Charting alone, is pure astrology and snake oil. Prices primarily follow 3 things: Fundamentals - Catalysts - Macro" (2026-03-05); "you look at numbers rather than narrative" (2025-12-31)。但认知分两层: 价值判断只看数字; 时机判断要懂会计光学/算法/期权链怎么读数字 (META BBB 税基数效应触发 HFT 买入, 2026-01-13)。TA 唯一合法用途 = 下跌时找入场点 (2026-04-23)。

**B9. 论点自立, 公开记账, 市场是最终裁判**
原话: "Arguments should stand on their own, not based on who it came from" (2025-10-26); "When a discussion shifts to personal attacks, it probably means your thesis is not holding up" (2025-10-27); "A thesis should live and die based on merit, not the authority of who it comes from" (2026-04-20); "Markets are the biggest arbiter of truth" (2026-04-12)。配套行为: 公开全部浮亏 + lesson learned (2025-10-18); 不卖订阅不收钱 ("I make money through markets", 2025-12-21); "I do put my money where my mouth is" (2026-02-03); 不做 copy-trading、不公布卖出时点 (2026-04-22)。

**B10. 高确信 = 已去风险 + 正在放量**
原话: "A stock is high conviction when they are fundamentally de-risked and are going from that point to scale" (2025-12-21)。去风险事件 (MSFT $17B 合同 + 高于市价融资) 把投机升级为高确信 (NBIS $50 投机不重仓 → $90+ 重仓, 2025-09-19)。距终端客户跳数决定级别: 直连 Mag7 > 两跳三跳 (AAOI vs POET, 2025-12-11); 反向传染同理 (APLD 距 OpenAI 两跳也避开, 2026-01-24)。

**B11. 时间线提前 = alpha, 时间线太远 = 陷阱**
原话: "you find any indicators timelines have been pushed up a lot (CPO got pushed up), then that's alpha" (2026-05-11)。CPO 量产 2027→H2 2026 提前 = 重仓信号; 2029+ 题材 (MicroLED/量子点) "太早进场会横盘一年被洗出去" (2026-04-11)。设备商例外规则: MBE/光刻设备在客户 capex 研发期就确认收入, 比纯器件公司早 2-3 年吃周期 (ALRIB/VECO, 2026-04-11/28)。

**B12. 事件交易: 被 telegraph 的已 priced in, surprise 才有 alpha; 战争默认 bullish**
原话: "rule of thumb is War is bullish, as long as it's not on your own territory" + 73% 二战后冲突一年后股市为正 (2026-02-28)。Venezuela (surprise) vs Iran ("one of the most telegraphed attacks in history") 的对照处理: 前者当日抢二阶受益, 后者事前对冲、事后把涨爆的对冲轮换进被错杀的高 beta (2026-02-28, 2026-03-10)。

### 3. 证据层 — 分级、归一化、噪音清单

**E1. 证据分级 (从他的用词还原, 归纳)**
- 确认级: 已签 take-or-pay 合同 + 对手方有钱 ("no counterparty risk from Mag7 (infinite balance sheets)", 2025-12-11); SEC 一级文件原文措辞; 管理层 transcript 原话 ("sold out really until the end of 2027" LITE 2026-03-17; "The thing that keeps me up at night most is: Substrates" LITE CEO 2026-03-20; "We do not look at competitors when demand far outstrips supply" SIVE 2026-05-29); 现货价格序列带日期; 有文号的政府文件; 股东名册。
- 高置信推断级: 官网变更/图片 slide/RFQ 匹配/两跳映射 — 必须标 "likely / high confidence"。
- 纯推测级: 自标 "speculative modeling, do not treat as fact" (2025-12-23 Maia BOM) / "(All speculative, very confidential BOM)" (AEHR 客户推测)。
- 核心公式: **证据成色 = 数字 × 对手方信用** — 同样的 backlog, 对手方是 OpenAI 则降级为可蒸发应收 (ORCL $300B Stargate, 2025-12-11)。

**E2. 卖方/第三方处理规则**
卖方研报 = 中级: 双源对比取保守源 ("I'd probably go off Morgan Stanley projections instead, a lot more grounded", 2026-02-27); 当 TAM 脚手架和名单池用 (GS CPO $0→$91B), 在机构建仓前抢跑; 警惕撤回 (Benchmark "Dec 8 高确信 → Dec 23 撤回"); "只读研报里没公开过的 nuance, 然后自己推 MC" (2026-05-02)。行业研究 (LightCounting/TrendForce/Yole) 默认 "likely understating" 需交叉。公司自报数据降一级 (NBIS 白皮书 near-100% 利用率 → 模型仍按 85% 取值)。专家对话只用于方向不用于数字 (2026-02-09)。
